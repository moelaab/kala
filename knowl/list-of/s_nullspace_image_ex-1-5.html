Let \(A=\left[\begin{matrix} 3 \amp 3 \cr 2 \amp -3 \cr -4 \amp 4\cr \end{matrix}\right]\text{,}\) \({\bf b}=\left[\begin{matrix} 6 \cr -7 \cr \end{matrix}\right]\text{,}\) and \({\bf c}=\left[\begin{matrix} 5 \cr -5 \cr \end{matrix}\right]\text{.}\) Define \(T({\bf x})=A{\bf x}\text{.}\)
Select true or false for each statement.
  1. The vector \({\bf b}\) is in the kernel of \(T\)
  2. The vector \({\bf c}\) is in the range of \(T\)
Solution.
\(A{\bf b}=\left[\begin{matrix} 3 \amp 3 \cr 2 \amp -3 \cr -4 \amp 4\cr \end{matrix}\right] \left[\begin{matrix} 6 \cr -7 \cr \end{matrix}\right]= \left[\begin{matrix} -3 \cr 33 \cr -52 \cr \end{matrix}\right] \ne {\bf 0}\text{,}\)\({\bf b}\not\in \ker(T)\text{.}\)\(T\)\({\mathbb R}^3\)\({\bf c}\in{\mathbb R}^2\text{,}\)\({\bf c}\not\in\)\((T)\text{.}\)
